## ha-lan-proxon

The home network connection (`custom_components/proxon_control/local.py`, `local_cert.py`) follows the protocol details worked out
by the ha-lan-proxon project (https://github.com/Fummy1990/ha-lan-proxon): the uNabto packet format over UDP port 5570, the queries
for reading and writing set points and data points, and the way the certificate is signed by the Proxon portal.
